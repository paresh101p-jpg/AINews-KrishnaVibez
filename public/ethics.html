EDITORIAL STANDARDS & CODE OF ETHICS

Editorial Integrity, Fact-Checking & Transparency Guidelines

1. Verification & Fact-Checking Standards

Every technical claim, benchmark comparison (MMLU, SWE-Bench, GSM8k), silicon specification (TFLOPS, memory bandwidth, TDP), and robotics throughput figure published by AINews must be cross-verified against official lab technical reports, peer-reviewed preprints, SEC filings, or direct on-record statements.

2. Corrections Policy

AINews is committed to immediate and transparent corrections. If a factual or technical error is identified in any published dispatch:

  • The error is corrected in the article body immediately.
  • A distinct, italicized Correction Notice is appended with the exact date, time, and explanation of what was amended.
  • Readers can request a correction by emailing corrections@ainews.krishnavibez.in with primary source documentation.

3. Synthetic & AI Tooling Disclosure

As a technology publication reporting on cutting-edge AI, AINews uses advanced synthetic data tools, NLP indexing, and automated pipelines to process high-volume research papers, corporate releases, and benchmark leaderboards. All final reporting, structure, and editorial conclusions are curated and audited under human editorial direction.

4. Conflicts of Interest & Independence

AINews maintains 100% editorial independence. We do not accept paid product reviews or sponsored rankings masquerading as investigative reports. When our coverage involves commercial partners or network affiliates, clear disclosures are rendered inline.
