Privacy Policy

1. Our Commitment to Reader Privacy

AINews (ainews.krishnavibez.in), published by Krishna Vibez Network, respects and protects the personal privacy of our global readers. This Privacy Policy details our practices concerning data collection, analytics, advertising disclosures, and reader rights under GDPR, CCPA, and global privacy frameworks.

2. Information We Collect

We believe in data minimization. When you browse AINews, we collect only standard server access logs and aggregated analytics to maintain website stability, performance, and security. We do not sell, rent, or trade readers' personal data.

  • Standard Log Data: IP address (anonymized), browser user-agent, operating system, and page request timestamps.
  • Analytics Data: Aggregated, non-personally identifiable traffic patterns via Google Analytics 4 (GA4).
  • Editorial Inquiries: If you contact us via email, your email address is used solely to respond to your specific editorial query or correction request.

3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

AINews uses cookies solely for essential functional operations, performance measurement, and anonymous analytics. You can disable cookies at any time through your browser settings without restricting access to our journalism.

4. Advertising & Google Services

We may display standard contextual advertisements via Google AdSense and participate in Google News Reader Revenue Manager. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on prior visits to this website. Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

5. Reader Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Under applicable data protection laws, readers have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of their personal information. To exercise any data privacy rights, please contact our Data Protection Desk at editorial@ainews.krishnavibez.in.
